The railroad through Truckee

The railroad is the oldest infrastructure in town and the hardest to learn about. A horn sounds at three in the morning and there is no way to learn what it was; the federal record says about fifteen trains cross Donner Pass every day, and the railroad publishes nothing. This site collects what can be known: what passed, how the passenger train is running, what governs the downtown crossing, how the line got here, and who built it.

Built by a Truckee resident from public records, every number traceable to its source. A sibling of truckee-flights and truckee-i80.

Data this site collects

  • Zephyr punctuality: scheduled versus actual times at the Truckee station, polled half-hourly, accumulating in the open in the repository.
  • The crossing's federal record: the Bridge Street inventory entry, pulled monthly, with every change logged.
  • Per-train log: planned. A calibrated home sensor will time, count, and measure the sound of every passage, publishing numbers only. Until then the freight record is the federal count, and this site says so rather than guessing.
