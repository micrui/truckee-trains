The Big Boy

Union Pacific's Big Boy locomotive crossed Donner Pass and stopped in Truckee in July 2024, the first of its type ever to do so. A return visit was scheduled for April 2026 but was cancelled because of snow. Here is what happened and how to find out when it will come again.

What it is

Union Pacific No. 4014, called the Big Boy, is the largest operational steam locomotive in the world. It was built in 1941 with a 4-8-8-4 wheel arrangement, meaning four leading wheels, two sets of eight driving wheels, and four trailing wheels. Union Pacific's steam program returned it to service in May 2019.

Source: Union Pacific steam program

When it came through Truckee

On July 14, 2024, No. 4014 crossed Donner Pass on an excursion from Roseville, California to Sparks, Nevada, running over the former Southern Pacific route. It was the first locomotive of its type ever to cross Donner Pass. That day it made brief public stops, called whistle-stops, at Colfax and Truckee, following public display days in Roseville on July 12 and 13.

A return was scheduled for April 12, 2026, as part of a coast-to-coast tour that began April 10, 2026 in Sacramento. A 15-minute Truckee whistle-stop was planned for 4:45 pm. A heavy snow forecast forced the train to reroute through the Feather River Canyon, and the Colfax and Truckee stops were cancelled.

Enthusiast sources report that Union Pacific steam locomotives 844 and 3985 crossed Donner together in 1999 and in 1981. Those dates come from enthusiast sources, not from news reports or railroad records, so treat them as unverified.

Sources: Railfan & Railroad, 2024 tour · Railfan & Railroad, the crossing · Union Pacific 2024 release (whistle-stop list) · Trains, 2026 reroute · Truckee Chamber, cancelled stop

How to know when it is coming back

No future tour is currently announced. Union Pacific publishes tour schedules at its steam schedule page and runs a live GPS tracker during tours. Its free email list, the Steam Club, notifies members first. This site's planned sensor log, a calibrated microphone and receiver near downtown, will record the passage when it comes.
