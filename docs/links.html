Watching the trains

The people who already watch this line, and the tools they use. Every link below was verified working; dead and paywalled resources are left off.

Live

  • Truckee Donner Railroad Society train cam: live view from a caboose beside the line, with railroad radio audio.
  • Downtown Truckee webcam: cycles views including the Bridge Street crossing.
  • Railroad radio, Donner Pass: the live dispatch and road channels for the line, received in Truckee. Listening is free; the feed's terms restrict rebroadcast, so it is linked here rather than embedded.
  • Amtrak's live map (or this site's Truckee log of it).

History and archives

  • Donner Summit Historical Society, the Heirloom: eighteen years of monthly local history, much of it railroad, free PDFs with an article index.
  • Truckee-Donner Historical Society: the town-side railroad story and a large online photo library.
  • Pacific Southwest Railway Historical Society: the best free chronology of the Donner route.
  • Historic employee timetables: Southern Pacific Sacramento Division, 1893 to 1980.
  • California State Railroad Museum library: the deep archive, including Southern Pacific corporate records; by appointment.
  • The abandoned 1868 summit track, now the tunnel hike.

Community

  • Truckee Donner Railroad Society: the local society, museum, and rotary snowplow display.
  • Trainorders: where the people who know this line hour by hour post about it; reading is free.
