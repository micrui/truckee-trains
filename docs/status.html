The Zephyr at Truckee

Amtrak's California Zephyr stops in Truckee twice a day: train 5 westbound toward the Bay, train 6 eastbound toward Chicago. This page logs scheduled against actual times at the Truckee station and accumulates the punctuality record. It updates through the day and settles by the following morning. Last rendered 2026-09-11 19:52 UTC.

Where is it right now: live position of train 5 westbound and train 6 eastbound, or the national live map.

Tickets: book at amtrak.com and enter Truckee, CA (TRU) as your station; Amtrak's booking page does not accept a prefilled link.
