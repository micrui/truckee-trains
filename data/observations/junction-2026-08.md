# Junction configuration observation, August 2026

Observed on the ground by the site maintainer, August 2026, at the Bridge Street
railroad crossing area, downtown Truckee:

- Bridge Street / Donner Pass Road: operates as an all-way stop; stop signs present
  on three of the four approaches.
- Bridge Street / West River Street: no traffic control.
- Pedestrian crossings near the railroad crossing: unmarked.
- Summer high-season behavior observed: drivers stopping without a sign, pedestrians
  crossing without crosswalks, queues on all approaches.

Checkable on site. Re-observe after any Town changes to the intersections
(Reimagine Bridge Street includes signals at both).
